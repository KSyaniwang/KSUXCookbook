Ritual of Discovery: A UX Analysis

From fragmented curation to a seamless selection process. How we redesigned the K-Beauty discovery journey.

Product Context

Primary Purpose

Korean Skincare is a curated marketplace designed to bridge the gap between complex Korean skincare routines and Western consumers. The platform aims to demystify active ingredients and multi-step rituals through personalized curation.

Target Users

  • check_circle The Skintellectual: Knowledgeable users looking for specific formulations.
  • check_circle The Novice: Overwhelmed by options, seeking guided discovery.

Why focus on the Discovery Flow?

Data shows that 72% of users drop off before reaching a Product Detail Page (PDP). The current "Discovery" phase—moving from Home to Category Listing—is where the brand promise of "simple curation" is tested.

By optimizing this specific flow, we can increase the Cart Conversion Rate by ensuring users feel confident in their product choices early in the funnel.
